DETACH
======

How it works in simulation
- Trigger: RViz DETACH button or   ros2 topic pub -t 3 /fleet/detach std_msgs/msg/Int32 "{data: k}"   (k = physical drone id)
- Controller (dissipative node), two modes:
    reconfig_mode:=ocp      (best, R0111-R0114): re-solve with a pre-built n-1 OCP, survivors keep their true rim azimuths
                            (attach geometry is a runtime parameter). Settled tilt ~1 deg, survivor error ~1 mm.
    reconfig_mode:=network  (node default): hand over to the spring-damper network, it re-spaces the survivors and
                            raises their tension share, then hands back to the n-1 OCP once still. Tilt 26-48 deg.
- Physical release: the node publishes /drone_k/detach -> gz bridge -> the Gazebo DetachableJoint breaks. Simultaneous
  with the controller change, which is why the transient is clean.
- Freed drone: network mode = rise 0.6 m straight up and hold; OCP mode = HOLD WHERE IT DETACHED (a rod length above the
  rim, R0113/R0114), no cable term. LAND descends it with the fleet at land_vel. Wesley 2026-09-24: hold where detached;
  a later handover to another student's controller starts from that hold.



Steps to fly it on the rig (magnets already on the tethers; a helper switches them)
1. Code (2026-09-24, card docs/experiments/2026-09-24_detach_land_fix.md):
   a. DONE: real_dissipative_launch.py reconfig_mode:=ocp reconfig_hold_s:=1.5 detach_magnet:=true (node publishes
      /drone_k/magnet OFF on the same tick as the resize; default false = helper toggles by hand).
   b. BUILT, Gazebo proof pending (detach_ocp_n4 x2): touchdown counts only the drones still on the load, the survivors'
      reference freezes at the stall, /fleet/landed waits until the freed drone is below 0.15 m (15 s cap, WARN).
      Root cause of the old abort: the departed drone's descent kept the touchdown test open and the survivors were
      ground into the floor to 60 deg. real_io_launch.py detach:=true shows the DETACH button.
   c. tools/prebuild_planner.py once before an ocp flight (the node builds the n-1 solver at start otherwise).
2. Which drone: 4 -> 3 with four airframes (the safe case). With three, 3 -> 2 leaves the disc hanging from two rim points
   = a pendulum about their chord; only defensible if the two survivors are at 3 and 9 o'clock, in hover, low.
3. Protocol, in hover first, ~0.6 m load height, freed drone holds where it is (~0.95 m):
   rio drone0_serial:=/dev/QUAD1 drone1_serial:=/dev/QUAD2 drone2_serial:=/dev/QUAD3 drone3_serial:=/dev/QUAD4 num_drones:=4 detach:=true magnet_initial:=ON
   rdiss num_drones:=4 load_mass:=0.86 cable_len:=0.47 thrust_ratio:=24 lift_ramp_vel:=0.15 load_traj:=hover reconfig_mode:=ocp detach_magnet:=true
   mdc && tools/preflight.py --drones 4 --real --planner dissipative_controller --max-ground-z 0.5
   operator counts down, publishes /fleet/detach k on "go", helper switches magnet k OFF on the same word (within 1 s).
   Command FIRST if they cannot be simultaneous: survivors take the load at once and the still-attached drone only tugs
   its rim for a moment. Release first is worse: the freed drone's tracker still models a cable pull it no longer feels
   (~3 m/s^2 outward-up) and the load loses a third of its lift with nobody compensating.
4. Then LAND with all drones; then detach during a circle at 0.2 m/s.
5. Read back after: survivor tilt, load height dip, freed-drone climb; compare with R0111-R0114 (~1 deg, ~1 mm).
