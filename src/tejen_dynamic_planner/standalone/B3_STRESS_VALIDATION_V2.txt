R6.3B.3 v2 - stronger dynamic commissioning scenarios
======================================================
31 AUG 2026

Why v2 exists
-------------
The user's native B.3 v1 suite passed 12/12, but visual inspection showed nearly
straight planning-drone paths. That can be correct time-aware behaviour, especially
when the planner safely waits or passes through the same space at another time, but the
first scenarios were not strong enough by themselves to prove meaningful conflict
resolution.

No planner algorithm or parameter is changed in v2.

New stress contract
-------------------
Each stress scenario first runs the same planner in an empty world. Its sampled
unobstructed trajectory is checked against the fixed shared other-drone trajectory/tube.
The scenario is considered valid only if that nominal minimum simultaneous clearance is
strictly negative (< -1e-4 m). The obstacle-aware run must then reach the goal while
satisfying the existing continuous safety, C2 and authority contracts.

New cases
---------
- crossing_stress: slow perpendicular crossing at x=1.5. Direct y=0 C-space overlap
  from approximately 4.50 to 7.80 s.
- same_direction_stress: other drone starts 0.55 m ahead on the same centreline and
  moves slowly to x=3.65 over 16 s.
- two_drones_stress: slow opposing crossings at x=1 and x=2; both direct-path C-space
  overlaps are approximately 3.26 to 7.97 s.

The original three cases remain as useful time-awareness regressions.
