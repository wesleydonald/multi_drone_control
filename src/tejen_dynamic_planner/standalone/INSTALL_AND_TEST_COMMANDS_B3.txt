New B.3 tests at the end:

test_cooperative_mission preserves crossing, same_direction and two_drones.
test_cooperative_stress runs crossing_stress, same_direction_stress and
two_drones_stress. Every stress scenario first proves that the same planner's
unobstructed mission has negative sampled simultaneous clearance against the
configured other-drone tube. It then requires the obstacle-aware mission to reach
the goal with the normal continuous safety/C2/authority contracts.

Default planning radius in this package is 2.0 m. cooperative_mission_demo also
accepts --planning-radius <metres> for explicit sensitivity runs.
