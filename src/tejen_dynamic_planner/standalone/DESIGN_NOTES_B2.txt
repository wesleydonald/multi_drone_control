R6.3B.2 design notes - approved proposal after RMADER/MADER cross-check
=====================================================================

Transaction
-----------
  W_plan = immutable snapshot
      |
      v
  build local 4-segment candidate
      |
      v
  B.1 late-candidate check
      |
      v
  W_latest = newest snapshot
      |
      +--> certify old incumbent [finish_time, A]
      |
      +--> certify candidate [A, candidate_end]
      |
      v
  atomic runIfVersionCurrent(W_latest.version, replaceSuffix)
      |
      +--> current: commit
      |
      +--> changed: take ONE newer snapshot, recheck, retry commit once
                    second version race => reject

Continuous convex checking
--------------------------
For a cubic polynomial subinterval [ta,tb], dt=tb-ta:

  B0 = p(ta)
  B1 = p(ta) + dt/3 * v(ta)
  B2 = p(tb) - dt/3 * v(tb)
  B3 = p(tb)

These are the exact cubic Bezier control points of the same subcurve. Split at
B-spline knots / committed-piece boundaries before applying the formula.
The entire continuous subcurve lies inside conv{B0,B1,B2,B3}.

Cooperative obstacle tube
-------------------------
  O(t) = p_committed(t)
         (+) physical obstacle AABB
         (+) tracking-error AABB
         (+) ego AABB

Initial tracking-error half-extents:
  [0.05, 0.05, 0.05] m

Physical and tracking extents remain separate in the data model so the 5 cm
commissioning assumption can later be replaced by measured data.

Failure semantics
-----------------
LATEST_CANDIDATE_UNSAFE:
  candidate rejected; incumbent has separately been certified safe up to A.

INCUMBENT_PREFIX_UNSAFE:
  newest world invalidates the already-committed interval before A. B.2 reports
  the condition and does not claim normal incumbent continuation is safe.
  Emergency shortening/stop/hover behavior is deliberately deferred to a later
  approved execution-supervisor increment.

WORLD_CHANGED_DURING_FINAL_RECHECK:
  the one permitted bounded retry also lost the version race. Reject without
  authority mutation; do not loop.

Atomicity
---------
VersionedWorld::runIfVersionCurrent() holds the world mutex while comparing the
version and executing the short replaceSuffix() action. Therefore an update
cannot land between the final version comparison and authority mutation.

Scope limitation
----------------
This local version transaction protects against newly arrived world information.
It does not prove safety against messages/trajectory changes that have not yet
arrived due to network delay. That stronger problem is the purpose of RMADER's
Delay Check and is outside B.2 under the present one-active-replanner assumption.

Authority-time validity after recheck
-------------------------------------
B.1 rejects a candidate if optimization itself finishes after A. B.2 adds a
second timing gate inside the final atomic commit action because collision
rechecking also consumes time:

  authority_decision_time >= A
    => LATE_CANDIDATE_REJECTED_AFTER_RECHECK

Therefore a candidate is never committed to a splice epoch that has passed while
Check/Recheck was running.
