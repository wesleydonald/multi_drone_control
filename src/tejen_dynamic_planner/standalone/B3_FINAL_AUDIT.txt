TEST POLICY
-----------
Existing unit test test_receding_horizon.cpp deliberately keeps its explicit 1.0 m test
configuration. This preserves regression coverage of the previously validated local setup.
The B.3 end-to-end CTests invoke cooperative_mission_demo without an override, so they
exercise the new 2.0 m default.

RADIUS-SWEEP INTERPRETATION
---------------------------
The selected 2.0 m value is an engineering compromise, not a universal optimum. The sweep
showed that local feasibility and trajectory quality can depend on environment/time geometry.
R_a=1.5 m specifically failed the initial two_drones_stress plan with
PADDED_CLOSEST_PARTIAL_FINAL_CHECK_FAILED, while 2.0 m succeeded in that tested geometry.
R_a=3.0 m effectively covered the complete 3 m mission and therefore ceased to be a useful
representative rolling-horizon setting.
