R6.3C.1a v2 - ROS Humble CMake signature fix
=============================================

User-native colcon configure exposed one ROS-package-only CMake integration bug in v1:
`dynamic_planner_reference_message` used the keyword form of target_link_libraries
(`PUBLIC ...`), while ROS Humble's `ament_target_dependencies()` subsequently uses the
plain form internally. CMake forbids mixing the two signatures for one target.

Fix:
- changed only tejen_dynamic_planner/CMakeLists.txt linkage for
  dynamic_planner_reference_message to the plain target_link_libraries signature;
- bumped tejen_dynamic_planner project/package version 0.1.0 -> 0.1.1;
- no planner algorithm, reference-window math, ROS message mapping, MPC, tests, or
  frozen B.4 source behaviour was changed.

The stale EGO/cmake_utils/pose_utils prefix warnings in the user's shell are unrelated
to this failure. They can be cleaned later; they are not the reason C.1a failed to
configure.
