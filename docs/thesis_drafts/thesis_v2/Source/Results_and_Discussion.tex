Each section answers one question of Table~\ref{tab:rq_map}: it opens with the question, its baseline
and its bar, and closes with the answer. Section~\ref{sec:res_carry} answers RQ1 (carrying, with the steady
payload errors in Section~\ref{sec:res_steady}), Section~\ref{sec:sim_detach} RQ2 and
Section~\ref{sec:sim_attach} RQ3; Section~\ref{sec:ledger} keeps the ledger of what the twin predicted, and
Section~\ref{sec:discussion} discusses the answers in the same order.

\paragraph{Scope.} The project plan's secondary question, whether a decentralised dissipative network or the
trajectory-based planner better balances robustness and tracking on the same plant and trackers, was
answered on the earlier simulation plant (a 0.6\,kg disc and then the ring on 0.5\,m rods with a linear
thrust map) before the rig twin existed. On that plant the planner was better in tracking, in detach and in
attach, and the network is not re-flown on the twin: Appendix~\ref{sec:app_network} records the method, the
comparison and the measured reasons. Every result in this section is the planner's.
