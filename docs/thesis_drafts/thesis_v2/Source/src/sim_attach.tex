\subsection{Attach and the rejoin}
\label{sec:sim_attach}

This section answers RQ3: does an attach by OCP resize fold a newcomer in with a small transient, small
enough for the transport to continue? The baseline is the fleet before the weld; the bar is the
ring's tilt back under 2$^\circ$ within 3\,s of the weld (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). The network attach of the
earlier plant, once the baseline here, is in Appendix~\ref{sec:app_network}.

Of the three stages (a network attach, the attach by OCP resize, and the full rejoin mission with the
collaborator's approach controller), only the attach by OCP resize with the newcomer flying its own approach
has been flown in the rig twin (the tip loop below and the LAND tests of Section~\ref{sec:sim_landing}), and
none on the rig yet.

\subsubsection{The approach: steering the magnet tip}

The approach flies the newcomer's body to its target plus an assumed rod length, so a wrong length parks
the magnet tip above or below the plate; in the collaborator's earlier attempts on the rig the magnet came over
the plate but too high. A slow, bounded loop on the measured tip height therefore corrects the body goal,
integrating only while the newcomer holds still over the plate and freezing inside a 5\,mm deadband, on a
contact and on an invalid tip measurement. In the rig twin, with the assumed rod length 4\,cm wrong in either
direction, the tip parks 4\,cm high or low with the loop off; with the loop on (two runs per sign) it comes
within 1\,cm of its setpoint 1.2--2.1\,s after the hold begins and stays within 0.1--0.6\,cm
(Figure~\ref{fig:tip_feedback}).

% \paragraph{Mass and rod length during an attach.} On the attach demonstration the controller was told
% a wrong mass or rod length while the simulated plant kept the true ones (two runs each). Telling it
% the payload is 25\,\% lighter, or the rods 10\,\% shorter, than they are was absorbed: both runs
% completed. Telling it the payload is 25\,\% heavier over-lifted the payload to 0.96\,m, and telling it
% the rods are 10\,\% longer dropped it to 0.17\,m; both aborted within about 10\,s of the weld. The
% envelope is asymmetric. An underestimate is made up by the height trim, while an overestimate plans
% more pull than the payload needs. On the rig the payload is weighed and the rods measured, and a
% doubtful value is entered low.
