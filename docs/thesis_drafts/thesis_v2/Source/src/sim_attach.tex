\subsection{Attach and the rejoin}
\label{sec:sim_attach}

This section answers the join half of RQ1: can a free UAV weld onto the carried ring and be folded in
by OCP resize with a transient small enough for the transport to continue? The baseline is the network
attach at the same weld; the bar is the ring's tilt back under 2$^\circ$ within 3\,s of the weld
(Section~\ref{sec:metrics}).

The attach was developed in three stages: a network-based attach, the attach by OCP resize, and the
full rejoin mission with the collaborator's approach controller. The attach by OCP resize, with the
newcomer flying its own approach, has been flown in the rig twin (the measured-tip loop below, and the
LAND tests of Section~\ref{sec:sim_landing}); the full rejoin mission has not, and none of the three has
yet been flown on the rig.

\subsubsection{The approach: steering the magnet tip}

The approach flies the newcomer's body to its target plus an assumed rod length, so an assumed length
that is wrong parks the magnet tip above or below the plate instead of on it. The collaborator's
earlier attempts on the rig showed this: the magnet came over the plate but too high. A slow, bounded
loop on the measured tip height therefore corrects the body goal. It integrates only while the
newcomer holds still over the plate, and it freezes inside a 5\,mm deadband, on a contact and whenever
the tip measurement is invalid. In the rig twin, with the assumed rod length 4\,cm wrong in either
direction, the tip parks 4\,cm high or low with the loop off; with the loop on, in two runs per sign,
the tip comes within 1\,cm of its setpoint 1.2--2.1\,s after the hold begins and stays within
0.1--0.6\,cm (Figure~\ref{fig:tip_feedback}).
