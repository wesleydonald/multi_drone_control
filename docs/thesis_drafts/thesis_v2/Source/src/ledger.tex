\subsection{What the twin predicted}
\label{sec:ledger}

Every rig flight from 14 October is predicted by the rig twin before it flies: the twin flies the same
configuration on the same code tree, and its range over its runs, widened by the rig's own pre-event noise,
is the band the rig value is then scored against. The band rule, the scoring (hit or miss, the band's width,
and the ratio of the rig value to the twin's midpoint) and the two naive predictors the ledger is compared
with (``the rig equals the twin'' and ``the rig equals its previous flight of the same cell'') are stated
in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}. The 7--8 October flights, flown before the rule, appear as agreement, not
as scored predictions. [Table and figure: the ledger, one row per rig flight, after 14 October.]
