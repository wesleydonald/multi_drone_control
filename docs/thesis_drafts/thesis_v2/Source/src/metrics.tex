\subsection{Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}

All metrics are computed from the logged motion-capture poses of the payload and the UAVs, with the
events (TAKEOFF, the push, DETACH, LAND) taken from the run's event log.

\begin{description}
    \item[Height error] $e_z = z_L - z_L^{\mathrm{ref}}$, positive with the payload high. In hover, it
    is averaged over the 15\,s before LAND.
    \item[Horizontal error] $\|\mathbf{e}_{xy}\|$, the distance between the payload's centre and its
    reference in the horizontal plane. On a trajectory, it is averaged over the first lap or the
    whole figure, and its maximum is reported as well.
    \item[Payload tilt] The angle between the ring's axis and the vertical. Its mean over the hover and
    its peak after an event are reported.
    \item[Heave] The peak-to-peak height of the payload over the hover window.
    \item[Disturbance response] For a constant force applied for a fixed time, the displacement along
    the force relative to its mean over the 5\,s before. The steady value is its mean over the last
    5\,s of the force. The peak is its maximum while the force acts, and the overshoot its largest
    excursion the other way after the force ends. The time to recover is the time after which it
    stays within 1\,cm.
    \item[Stiffness] The applied force divided by the steady displacement with no integral action.
    \item[Detach] The peak payload tilt after DETACH, the time until the tilt stays under 2$^\circ$, and
    the payload's height loss.
    \item[Attach] The peak payload tilt after the weld, the payload's height loss (dip) after the
    weld, and whether the tilt returns under 2$^\circ$ within 3\,s; for the full rejoin mission, also
    the tilt at the detach, the mean tilt over the orbit and the time from TAKEOFF to landed.
    \item[Carried fraction] On the rig, where rod tensions are not measured: the total vertical force
    the UAVs put into their rods, computed from each UAV's throttle through the identified thrust map
    and its measured attitude, divided by the payload's weight.
    \item[Solver health] The number of planner solves that failed or did not converge.
\end{description}

Every Gazebo case is flown at least twice and reported as the mean, with the number of runs.
