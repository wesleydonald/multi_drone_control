\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

Cooperative transport of a cable-suspended payload by several UAVs usually assumes a fixed team.
This thesis asks whether a centralised controller can let a UAV leave or join a payload-carrying team in
flight. A model predictive planner with per-UAV tracking flies unmodified Betaflight UAVs and resizes its
optimal control problem when the team changes. On hardware, four UAVs carried a 0.86\,kg ring within 3\,cm
of its path on average, and three carried it through five of six commanded releases; every ring lost began
with a magnet letting go. In simulation, the support margin ordered the release transient. [Join: after the
rig day.]
