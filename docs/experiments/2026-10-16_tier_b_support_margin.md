## 1. Question

With the measured pull, does the ring's tilt after an unannounced release grow as the support margin the survivors
leave shrinks, and does the planned pull still lose at a smaller margin (thesis plan C2-B, "geometry decides")?

## 2. Hypothesis

Support margin m = r cos(g/2), r = 0.225 m, g = the survivors' largest gap (thesis plan 3.4; statics, Michael 2011).
Smaller m means a larger moment for the survivors to take over when the leaver goes, so a larger Delta-theta. From the
existing runs: 5-6 deg at 120 deg (m 112 mm) and 17-19 deg at 150 deg (m 58 mm), measured pull, model integral.
At 180 deg (m 0) the ring cannot hang level on three cables. Twin caveat: the rods are rigid and can push, so at 165
and 180 deg the twin may hold a ring that slack cables would not.

## 3. The one variable

The third survivor's azimuth S, so the gap. The leaver is always drone 1 at 90 deg ccw (plate 9). Two survivors stay at
30 (plate 11) and 270 (plate 3); the third sits at S, so g = S - 30. Everything else is the W4 base (`ta_a1_r1` =
`w4_da_release_hover`): hover at 1.0 m, measured pull, model integral, detection 0.06 m, departed_land and
departed_ground_idle, drop_and_land.yaml. RELEASE at 20.0 s (r1) or 20.5 s (r2), and LAND 20 s after it.
