# Twin R9: attach during the circle (gate for Thursday's rig R9)

## 1. Question
Does QUAD4 weld onto plate 9 and the fleet go on circling on four, when the attach happens during the circle:
first with the circle held for the approach (Thursday's first R9), then with the circle running (the second)?

## 2. Hypothesis
Held: the same as the hover R8b once the circle stops (weld, ring tilt peak within the R8b band of 1.7-4.3 deg),
then the circle resumes on four after the reconfiguration hold. Moving: the weld still happens; the moving plate
adds a radial tip swing, so the tip loop (z only, still hold) may not engage and the weld may come later.

## 3. The one variable
`attach_moving` (false: the circle is held from ATTACH through the reconfiguration hold; true, the default: the
circle keeps running). Everything else is the twin R8b of the Wednesday sheet (configs/experiments/twin_tf_weld_on.yaml:
tip loop on, weld radius 0.025, rig weld gate 0.05 m/s, tip noise 0.5 mm, newcomer switches on) plus the circle
r 0.5 m at 0.125 m/s after a 5 s hover (configs/rig/traj_hold.yaml). Configs: twin_r9_held.yaml, twin_r9_moving.yaml.

## 4. Baseline
The hover R8b (twin_tf_weld_on): R1404 / R1410 weld at 48.9 / 52.3 s, ring tilt peak MAGNET ON..LAND 1.7 / 4.3 deg,
closest newcomer-carrier body distance 0.45 m; the check run R1454 (10 Oct, rig-prep c2279cf) adds a third.
The three-carrier circle without an attach: R1228 / R1229 (w4_3915_orbit_0125).

## 5. Pass / fail numbers (per run; written 10 Oct before the runs)
- Weld: confirms if QUAD4 welds; falsifies if it does not weld within 60 s of reaching the hold, or the fleet aborts.
- Ring tilt peak, weld -1 s to weld +10 s: confirms <= 5 deg (G1 rejoin bar); falsifies > 8 deg.
- Carriers' dip (ring height drop in the same window): confirms <= 0.03 m; falsifies > 0.05 m.
- The circle runs again on four: confirms within 5 s of the reconfiguration hold's end (planner log); falsifies if it
  never resumes before LAND.
- Newcomer body to any carrier body, MAGNET ON to the weld: confirms >= 0.35 m (the rig stop rule); falsifies < 0.30 m.
- LAND: every drone landed and disarmed, no abort.

## 6. Repeats
