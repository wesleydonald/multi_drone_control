**Two things for Wesley before the bars freeze (push-back, said once):**
1. **S's residual bar (N6) cannot be scored at `plan_res_every: 0`.** At one iteration the publish gate never computes
   a warm tick's residual (`planner_solver.py`: the budget cut cannot fire when the iteration cap is 1), and
   `plan_res_every 0` stops the logged one too, so every published warm tick reads "published without one". Option
   (recommended): the lab-PC runs (the quality claim) fly `plan_res_every: 1`. With `plan_fast_refs` on, the residual
   is computed from the same iterate after the references went out (`finish_tick`), so the plan the trackers fly is
   unchanged; only the wall time per tick grows, which in the lab twin (RTF ~0.25, ticks on sim time) changes nothing.
   The ground-computer runs (the timing claim) keep `plan_res_every: 0`. Cost: none. The configs below are written
   as asked (`plan_res_every: 0` everywhere); **Wesley 30 Oct ~15:46: option taken**: the five `x3b_*_rt.yaml` fly `plan_res_every: 1`; `x3b_s{8,9}_chain4back_rt0.yaml` (`0`) are the tuf timing runs.
   Without it, N6 is reported "not measured at this setting" and the plan's quality rests on the closed-loop bars.
2. **"The same bars" means "the same verdicts".** The five-iteration chain claim MISSED N4 on position in both
   repeats (R2766/R2767: 6.36/6.35 mm against 5.66 mm; the last fold-in's return, not the iteration count) and the one
   exploratory 1-iteration chain read 6.3 mm (R2597). So the claim below is "1 iteration gives the verdict 5 iterations
   gives on every bar, and its numbers lie within S's it1-vs-it5 tolerances", not "every bar holds". The open-loop
   plan at 1 iteration and 8 carriers is NOT converged (S's Q6: residual p95 9.3e-3, references 7.3 / 14.5 mm off,
   feed-forward 0.60 m/s^2, all outside Q2-Q4), so this is a closed-loop claim only, and the thesis says so.

## 1. Question
At the setting that fits the 100 ms planner period on the ground computer (one SQP iteration per tick, the residual
only where the publish gate needs it, the fast references), does the 8-carrier ring flight give the same results as
the five-iteration claims; and what do nine carriers show (the reference paper's stated limit)?

## 2. Hypothesis
- **Real time, ground computer (tuf, the rig laptop).** The whole tick is the solve plus the mapped references; the
  residual was the rest. Bench B (24 Oct, idle, `res_every 1`, `results/analysis/s_bench_B_2026-10-24.json`) at 1
  iteration: n = 8 whole tick p50 / p95 82.5 / 83.1 ms of which the residual 33.3 / 33.7, latency (solve + references)
  49.2 / 49.7 ms; n = 9 105.9 / 106.4 ms, residual 43.0 / 43.2, latency 62.9 / 63.4 ms; n = 10 latency 77.5 / 78.1 ms.
  Predicted at `res_every 0`: whole tick p95 ~50 ms at 8, ~63 ms at 9, ~78 ms at 10, idle. In the twin's closed loop
  on the same laptop, higher (Gazebo and eight or nine trackers share the CPU), still under 100 ms at 8.
- **Quality, 8 carriers.** 1 iteration gives the same verdict as 5 on every S bar, within S's it1-vs-it5 tolerances:
  the hover ~1.3 mm and < 0.1 deg (R2464 at 1 iteration: tilt max 0.08 deg); the chain's releases 1.9-2.9 deg and
