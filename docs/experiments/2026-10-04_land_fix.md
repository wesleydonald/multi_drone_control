# land_fix (4 Oct 2026, overnight): the touchdown feedforward step and the grounded drone

## 1. Question

Wesley, 4 Oct night: "run a loop to fix what needs to be fixed". The two LAND aborts of the land
matrix (card 2026-10-04_land_matrix) have causes that `land_unwind` does not touch. Do the following
two fixes make every arm land?
- the trackers fly the planner's feedforward blend at load-down;
- a landed drone idles.

## 2. Hypothesis

**R1003 and R0981, survivors.** At load-down the planner already blends each drone's thrust and cable
feedforward to hover over LAND_BLEND_S (1.0 s). But in the same tick it publishes ff_active false, and
every tracker zeroes its cable term at once (`_load_grounded()`). That is a step: planned rod accel
(-4.0, -5.5) to 0, throttle 0.455 to 0.251, with the rod still acting. Pitch then oscillates to full
stick.
- Fix (planner knob `land_ff_ramp`): keep ff_active true until the blend ends, so the trackers fly the
  blend.
- Expected: no stick oscillation at load-down; tilt after load-down no higher than now.

**R0997, departed drone.** It lands first and keeps tracking a reference 10 cm below the floor at
throttle 0.45. Its roll oscillation grows and it flips.
- Fix (tracker knob `ground_idle`): once a drone has flown and is back within 3 cm of its resting height
  for 0.2 s, with the ring resting and its reference at the floor, it latches to armed idle (zero
  sticks, idle throttle) until the next ARM.
- Expected: no drone moves once it is down.

## 3. The one variable

Both knobs on, together: they act on different drones and moments of the same landing. Both default
off; Wesley flips them in the morning if this passes. Everything else is as in the land matrix:
`land_unwind` true (default), box at zero (default). Configs: `*_lf.yaml` = the matrix's `*_lu.yaml`
plus `land_ff_ramp: true`, `ground_idle: true`.

## 4. Baseline

Land matrix R0987-R1008: 16 of 18 clean, 2 aborts (R1003, R0997). Clean landings: off-reference 0.5-0.6 cm,
tilt after the blend 2.4-5.0 deg.

## 5. Pass / fail numbers

| metric (load-down to disarm, `tools/land_metrics.py`) | confirms | falsifies |
|---|---|---|
| LAND | every run lands, no abort | any abort |
| worst drone tilt after the blend | <= 5 deg | > 10 deg |
| off-reference push | <= 5 cm | > 8 cm |
| ring xy shift | <= 1 cm | > 2 cm |

The failing arms (map+, detach) fly first.

## 6. Repeats

2 per arm, 18 runs (Gazebo, floor start). 18 runs cannot prove a rate of a few per cent; the claim is
both mechanisms removed, read from the tracker logs (no stick oscillation at load-down, landed drones
idle).

## 7. Cost

About 80 min of laptop Gazebo.

## 8. New code?

Yes, small:
- planner: `land_ff_ramp`, one line at the land hold;
- tracker: `ground_idle`, `_ground_idle_update`, plus 4 unit tests;
- launch plumbing for both.

Not a new control law: a feedforward schedule and an idle on the floor. Critic not required by
CLAUDE.md. If it fails, the knobs stay off.
