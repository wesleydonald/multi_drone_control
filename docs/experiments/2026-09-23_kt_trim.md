# 2026-09-23 per-drone thrust-gain trim (hover-thrust estimation)

## 1. Question
Wesley: "I need a sure fix to the z-height / not picking up issue." With the "kT is
fixed" lock lifted (2026-09-23), does a slow, bounded, per-drone correction of the
throttle-to-acceleration gain, learned from the drone's own applied throttle in flight,
put the load at its target height whatever kT was typed, and whatever the load mass?

## 2. Hypothesis
With `kt_trim` on, a typed kT 10 % or 15 % off (SIL: hovers 0.45–0.50 / 0.46 m off) and
a +20 % unmodelled mass (0.511) all hover within 2 cm of the 0.60 target, the correct
case moves from 0.629 to within 2 cm of 0.60, settled tilt is not worse than off by more
than 1 deg, and the estimated gain lands within 3 % of the true one in the kT arms.

## 3. The one variable
Tracker parameter `kt_trim` (default **false** until the matrix passes; then Wesley's
word to flip). Per drone, at 50 Hz, the hover-thrust estimate

    kT_hat = (a_meas_z + g - a_cable_model_z) / (u_applied * R_33)

where `u_applied` is the throttle the tracker sent last tick, `R_33` the thrust axis'
vertical component, `a_cable_model_z` the planner's cable feedforward as applied (after
gate and slew), and `a_meas_z` the drone's vertical acceleration from mocap velocity
(first-order filtered, 0.3 s; the tracker model is v_dot_z = kT u R_33 - g + a_cable_z with
a_cable_z < 0 for a pull, so a heavier pull raises the estimate's numerator). At a steady
hover a_meas is 0 and the formula reduces to
"what gain makes the throttle I am flying produce the force I am holding". `kT_hat` is
first-order filtered with `kt_trim_tau` (8 s), bounded to `kT_typed * (1 +- kt_trim_max)`
(0.25), and replaces the fixed gain in `_effective_kT()` (the battery sag factor still
multiplies it). The MPC model's thrust gain follows, so both the vertical and the
horizontal components of the thrust vector are corrected.

Gates (update only when all hold, else the last estimate is held): armed and TAKEOFF
requested; the drone at least 0.15 m above its own arm height (its own state, not the
payload's); `u_applied` in [0.15, 0.6]; the cable feedforward soft-start complete
(`a_cable` as applied equals the planner's); |a_meas_z| < 1.5 m/s^2 (not in a transient);
no envelope fault. Reset to the typed gain on ARM. The value at LAND is logged and
reported as the measured kT for that airframe and pack (next launch's `thrust_ratio`).

Why this and not the three earlier attempts: the load-level Kalman filter could not see a
kT deficit (deleted today); a vertical additive integrator corrects only the vertical
component of a multiplicative error (critic on the z-integrator card); the INDI throttle
needed an IMU the rig does not have. A gain estimate from the applied throttle needs
only what the rig has (mocap and the command it sent), corrects the whole vector, and is
what PX4's hover-thrust estimator does.

Why it is not the UKF the lock was written against: one scalar per drone, filtered at
8 s, bounded to +-25 %, gated to steady flight, no coupling to the cable model's state.

Also in this change, Wesley's word 2026-09-23: `measure_rod_len` defaults to true on all
seven OCP launches with `cable_len` 0.5 as the initial estimate (closes decisions.md OPEN
line 12); real `thrust_ratio` stays 24, sim stays the derived value.

## Critic (2026-09-23)

(Reviewed the card with the formula as first written; the code already had the signs
of the tracker model, `kT = (a_meas_z + g − a_cable_z)/(u·R33)`, and the card was
corrected before this challenge landed. Points 1's sign remarks are therefore closed;
everything else stands.)

**1. Signs and convergence.** With the signs right, a converged kT_hat is the value that
makes the MPC's own vertical model consistent, using the same `a_cable` the MPC is given.
Correct arm: 36.75 (no-op, load stays ~0.629). kT arms: the true secant, IF the gate
opens. Mass +20 %: about 35.8 (−2.5 %, lower not higher: to deliver more force the model
must believe weaker motors), not "~44". If `a_cable_model` is wrong, kT_hat absorbs it
and the value reported at LAND is not a gain.
**2. Loop.** The secant-on-a-parabola coupling is negative and stable; in closed loop it
is an integrator on each drone's horizon error. Exception: `cable_source=measured`, where
`a_cable` already contains kT_hat and every value is a fixed point: refuse it.
**3. Secant vs tangent, and the newcomer.** The 2x tangent at the weld is unchanged. But
a free-flying newcomer learns the free-flight secant (29.5) and arrives at an n=4 weld
15 % low, i.e. 15 % over-thrust, while the tethered drones are 5 % high: a differential
vertical step at the most fragile moment. A detached drone relearns 29.5 over 8 s. The
±25 % bound, centred on one operating point, is not a safety bound across attach/detach.
**4. Gates.** The 0.15 m own-height gate never opens in the arms that matter: SIL drones
spawn at 0.83 m and the sagging hovers sit 0–6 cm above that (card 1's `lift_z0` bug
again). Near the stands `_effective_kT` flies `takeoff_thrust_ratio`. The "soft-start
complete" condition cannot be read in the tracker (gate × ff are multiplied in before
publishing; wire format locked at 12 fields). Exclude the takeoff spool. `a_meas` lags u
by 0.3 s and biases every throttle change; over 8 s it averages to ~0 anyway. LAND
descent leaves the gates open and pollutes the reported value.
**5. Mass arm hides a lateral failure.** kT_hat set by vertical consistency scales the
lateral thrust by the same factor: at +20 % the model's lateral balance is ~13 % off,
no lateral integrator, drones drift radially, rods steepen, z can pass with the geometry
wrong. Report radial drift and rod elevation; expected kT_hat ≈ 35.8.
**6. Differential gain.** Per-drone kT_hat absorbs per-drone `a_cable` error. Even n=3
ring: common mode by symmetry, the hover matrix cannot show a differential. 4/12/8,
3/12/9, one mis-typed rod, the rig's 4.3 cm origin offset: per-drone thrust biases = a
moment on the load (§6 `vel_ki` / §10 in multiplicative form). Add an uneven-layout arm
and report the kT_hat spread.
**7. Rig.** First flight starts from typed 24, trim frozen while the load rests: nothing
helps it lift. If the gate opens with the load resting, kT_hat absorbs the rod pull as
"weak motors" and breakaway is an over-thrust step. 6S sag: the 4S `kt_batt_v_*` derate
would stack on a trim already tracking sag: force the derate off when the trim is on.
The LAND value is taken on a sagged pack after touchdown: report the last steady hover
value with the pack voltage instead.
**8. Bands vs open problem 9.** "0.629 → 0.60 ± 0.02" and "kT_hat within 3 % of 36.75"
contradict each other: the correct arm is already consistent, the trim is a no-op there
and the 3 cm is the planner's. Split the claim: (a) kT_hat reaches the secant and each
drone's horizon error closes; (b) load height vs the correct-arm hover, 0.60 beside it.
**9. Cheaper.** (a) Shadow mode first: compute and log kT_hat without feedback; on the
rig that measures each airframe's kT on 6S directly, which may be the whole answer to
"typed 24". (b) Drop `a_meas`: `kT_hat = kT_typed · u_model_hover/u_applied` is the same
estimator without the differentiation. (c) Drop the `measure_rod_len` flip from this
card: second variable, and it feeds `a_cable` directly.
**10. The circle arm cannot discriminate**: `diss_circle_n3`-class lifts lose 4 of 10,
so "abort" fires ~64 % of the time with the trim inert.

Verdict: not ready as written. Gate on spool-complete not own height, refuse the
measured cable source, run shadow mode, split the bands, add an uneven-layout arm, and
cover the newcomer's free-flight learning.

### v2 first cut, R0375–R0382: the planned cable term drifts (VOID for the claim)
With the planner's applied feedforward as the estimator's cable term, the shadow value in
the correct arm read 36.48 (−0.7 %) and, applied, drifted: kt_hat 36.75 → 36.29 while the
load rose 0.607 → 0.669 m over 40 s (R0377/R0378); the kT +10 % arm converged to 36.5–37.0
(within 1 %) but the load was still rising at LAND (0.43 → 0.64, R0381/R0382), so the
hover-only mean 0.546 is a transient. Mechanism: the planned tension at node 0 carries the
planner's climb/descend intent (a load above target is planned to descend, so planned
pull < weight), and an estimator that reads it as the true pull believes the motors weaker
than they are, raises thrust, the load rises, the planned pull falls further: positive
feedback, slow. Fix (v3, same day): the planner publishes each drone's STATIC vertical
share (`/drone_<k>/cable_static_z`: the planned distribution normalised so the vertical
sum equals the load's weight, `static_cable_z`, unit-tested), and the trim uses that. At
a steady hover the static share is the true pull whatever the planner intends. The
critic's point 6 (per-drone share error on uneven layouts) becomes the 3/12/9 arm.
