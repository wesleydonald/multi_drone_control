## Critic

Critic agent, 12 Nov ~12:50 (read-only; pasted here by the lab chat). Verdict: **GO WITH CHANGES**. The hover and circle
trials are untouched by changes 1-2.

- F1. The card's evidence is wrong. R4305 flew with no pause (params/dissipative_planner.yaml:18, attach_pause false), and
  QUAD4 had already crashed at 119.5 s (an envelope fault at 75.6 deg tilt, launch.log:3169), lying inverted. The give-up at
  221.8 s flew nothing, and the ring tilt 60 s later is unexplained. R4299 is the real case of a figure-eight resuming
  beside a taken-back QUAD4 (zone c, moving with the ring): it was clean, 180 s more figure-eight, tilt peak 1.9 deg.
  (Checked by the lab chat: confirmed.)
- F2. The launch accepts the rig figure-eight with no hold: graph_attach never checks attach_pause. Fix: refuse
  contact_rig_fig8 unless attach_pause is true, plus a test.
- F3. Doors the hold leaves open (dissipative_node._attach_pause_wanted): traj_t > 0 is required; a take-back refused
  after a tilt-fault cut leaves his phase at LANDED_DISARMED and the figure-eight resumes; his LANDING ends the pause.
  Sheet rule: on the figure-eight, anything other than a weld means LAND at once (LAND freezes the path clock,
  planner_node.py:1709-1723, 1817).
- F4. A take-back flies the path's full-speed frame (`_take_back_tick`: `_ring_plan(t)` velocity and a full-rate horizon,
  dissipative_node.py:2955-2959, 3053-3060) while the ring slows or rests. With the hold, a take-back at a failed drop on
  the moving figure-eight (his give-up, online_join_planner.py:7655, 7667) leads the ring by up to 0.125 m/s and ~0.25 m
  with the magnet 3 cm over the net. No twin check covers it. Fix: scale the frame by the path rate, or fly that case in
  the twin. (Checked by the lab chat: the frame uses the full-speed plan; confirmed.)
- F5. The twin places QUAD4 1.9 m out, the card said 1.4 m. At 1.4 m the figure-eight's lobe brings carrier 3 to 0.46 m
  from QUAD4's mat at 1 or 5 o'clock (0.92 m at 1.9 m; the circle never closer than ~0.95 m). Fix: fly the rig start
  as the twin's, or his TAKEOFF only as the ring heads to the far lobe.
- F6. Position limit and mocap margin: the carriers reach x +-1.39 m, y -1.115/+0.745 m from the ring's rest point;
  LINEUP=true staging reaches rest y + 1.575 m. Sheet rule: the ring's rest within |x| <= 0.3 m, y in [-0.6, +0.1] m;
  1 and 5 o'clock only with LINEUP=gap.
- F7. A 0.75 m lap is 40.6 s, not ~30 s (load_trajectory.py:177-182); 12 laps = 8.1 min of path.
- F8. traj_laps is allowed on the rig in any mode when fig_8 and the switch are typed. Low risk.
- F9. Fly the same tree as the twin batch; re-fly the two figure-eight checks after the fixes; the reviewer, or a
