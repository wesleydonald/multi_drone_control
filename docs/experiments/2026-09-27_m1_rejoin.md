# M1 meets the G1 bars in Gazebo with a modified sim model of drone 3's magnet rod + four opt-in rejoin flags (claim), 2026-09-27

## Claim
The full M1 demo (four carriers on plates 1/3/5/9 lift the 0.86 kg ring and orbit at 0.125 m/s; drone 3 detaches,
steps out, flies Tejen's pickup mission, drops the ball into the ring's net, is handed back, rejoins by magnet weld
and OCP fold-in to n=4; LAND) meets the G1 bars with this configuration:
- SIMULATION MODEL change (not a controller change; test copy, not the shipped model): drone 3's magnet rod on
  universal joints at both ends (simulation_assets/models/x3_drone3_magnet_rod_partner_attached_uu.sdf; damping
  0.002 at the body end, 1e-4 at the tip). It works around a gz/DART weld artefact: with the shipped undamped ball
  joints the arm swung ~80 s before the at-rest weld gate passed (R0660/R0662), and the 2 g tip kept the spin left by
  the ball carry, which the gz weld copied onto the ring (0.78-2.27 rad/s steps, R0668-R0680; row M-yank). The two
  universal joints REMOVE the rod's twist freedom (drone 3's yaw is coupled to the ring while welded); with 1e-4 at
  the tip the effect most likely comes from removing that freedom, not from damping. Whether the real rod has this
  rotation freedom has not been checked;
- opt-in flags (config partner_attached_orbit_g3_uu.yaml): attach_t_start_new 0.1, attach_blend_balanced true,
  weld_velocity_clock sim, attach_datum_shift true; the partner mission's object magnet over ROS bridges. The flags
  have bench evidence (R0662-R0666, R0672-R0675) but did not stop the M1 yank (R0668-R0680, 15-36 deg) until the
  model change; one discovery run cannot separate their contribution in M1.

## Falsifier (GOALS.md G1, written before the claim runs)

at LANDED; detach peak ring tilt > 8 deg; orbit ring tilt mean > 3 deg; rejoin peak > 5 deg; carriers' dip > 0.03 m;
ring tilt not under 2 deg within 3 s of the weld; TAKEOFF -> landed > 140 s (sim). Thresholds are approximate
(Wesley): a run just outside with a clear reason is reported, not chased.
Repeats: 2 headless Gazebo runs (claim), then Wesley watches one in the GUI. Baseline: R0653 (M1 before the fixes)
and R0646. Discovery run R0684 is reported, not counted.
