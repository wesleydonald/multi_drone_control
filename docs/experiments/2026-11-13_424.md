## Risks (design studies results/analysis/424_design_2026-11-13/)
1. **Roll about the pair line.** Two opposite carriers give no moment about their line; only the ring's centre of mass
   below the cable ends (~2-5 cm lever) resists. Plates 3 and 9 are on that soft axis:
   - a contact press, a lift proof or downwash rolls the ring (estimate ~8 deg for his press);
   - one funnel alone tilts the pair 3-9 deg;
   - the 4-carrier ring already hangs 3.7-5.4 deg low on 9's side.
   The pair-hang roll check comes before flying.
2. **No pair has carried a ring on 1 m cables or the M2A ring on the real quads.** Pair throttle is ~0.71-0.75 against
   the 0.95 cap; on 28 Oct a pair sat on its 0.9 cap in two sinks. The pair magnets carry ~4.9 N each, and rig magnets
   have let go at 2.8-6.5 N.
3. **The two partners are blind to each other** (his instances do not share commitments). Separation is geometric only:
   ~0.24 m airframe-to-airframe with both over their plates.
4. **A single-partner flag left somewhere:** e.g. one proof folding both, or a LAND taking back one.
5. **The queued partner** (serial fold-in) flies the warm reference with no cable feedforward while it waits; it may sink
   until its cable goes slack. Untested.
6. **His MPC has never flown QUAD3.** kT 22 by default (QUAD4's); read QUAD3's Betaflight rates first.
7. **Laptop CPU with two of his stacks:** on 13 Nov ring_bridge states already arrived 290-395 ms late.
