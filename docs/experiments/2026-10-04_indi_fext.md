# indi_fext (4 Oct 2026, overnight): the trackers' cable term from the measured external force

Wesley, 3 Oct (overnight loop): compare literature-backed control-law changes for the ring's internal
and external model errors. INDI in the tracker (Smeur, de Croon & Chu 2016/2018; Tal & Karaman 2021)
is one of his candidates.

## 1. Question

If each tracker's model uses the external acceleration it measures (IMU specific force minus the
thrust it commanded, as Tal & Karaman's INDI outer loop estimates f_ext) instead of the planner's
planned cable pull, do the ring's height and xy errors go away?

## 2. Hypothesis

**Why it might.** The offset-free card (2026-10-04_offset_free_innovation) shows the planner is
unbiased and the error is the cascade not executing the plan. In SIL R0473 the planner's node-0
tension is 3.97 N against the rods' 4.16 N, and the tracker's model gets the planned 3.97.

A tracker that models the force it actually feels predicts its own motion correctly, so it follows
the planned drone positions. A drone-borne map error (the map arm) or wind on the drones lands in
f_ext and is cancelled at the drone.

What it cannot see: a force on the ring that the trackers do not feel through the rods. The rigid
rods transmit it, so they do feel it, but only after the ring moves.

**History (do not repeat blind).**
- The measured-force throttle (INDI-lite, the velocity loop) was removed on 23 Sep (decisions.md):
  - It never beat the classic loop on the moving load.
  - At gain 1 each drone became a stiff position servo and the network's approximate rod lengths
    slackened the rods (CURRENT_STATE section 4.4, R0244-R0272).
- This is the other path: `cable_source: measured` in the MPC tracker (Part 2c, still in the code),
  under the OCP planner with rigid rods. It was never flown on the twin.
- Its magnitude cap `CABLE_ACCEL_CAP` 6 m/s^2 was sized for the old sim ("~2x taut"). The twin's
  cable accel is 6.9-8.1 m/s^2 (R0841/R0868 acz/acxy), so the cap must be raised or it clips.

**Expected.**

| arm | z_ki 0 | expected |
|---|---|---|
| twin hold | +4.8 cm | within 1 cm |
| map offset (R0870) | z -3.0 cm, xy 3.8 cm, tilt 6.7 deg | z, xy within 1 cm, tilt back near 1.1 deg |
| 1 N push | 18 cm | no better than R0856 (the ring force is felt only through the rods) |

`cable_accel_cap` (default 6.0 = today's constant) set to 12 m/s^2 on these arms. Slew 25 m/s^2/s
and the IMU low-pass are unchanged. Everything else is pinned to each arm's config.

## Results (4 Oct night)

| run | arm | result |
|---|---|---|
| R0881 | SIL hover | +5.8 cm (R0473 +6.3): no change, as the critic predicted |
| R0896 | Gazebo map arm (drone 2 plant offset 0.215) | ring tilt 0.83 deg (R0870 6.7: the per-drone map error is cancelled at the drone); z +3.8 cm (R0870 -3.0), xy 3.1 cm (3.8) |

R0896 ended in a fleet abort 7 s into LAND: drone 3 tilt 74.8 deg, after the switch back to the
planned term with the ring down.

**Why the measured term is off.** On drones 2 and 3 it sat at the 12 m/s^2 cap: (5.8, 9.2, -5.2)
against the planned (3.7, 5.9, -4.0). The excess lies along minus body z, a thrust-sized error.

The likely cause is mass: the Gazebo IMU is on the 0.475 kg airframe link, while kT 35.2 is calibrated
for 0.55 kg with the rod. The IMU's specific force (T + F_joint)/0.475 minus kT*thr = T/0.55 leaves
T (1/0.475 - 1/0.55), about 2.6 m/s^2 at hover, plus a 16 % scale on the joint force. The rig has the
same structure (the FC's IMU on the airframe, the rod outside it).

A fix would scale the specific force by m_airframe/m_map before subtracting. Not built tonight.

As built (absolute thrust, mass-mismatched IMU), it is not safe to land.
