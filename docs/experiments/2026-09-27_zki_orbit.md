# Height integral through a level orbit (opt-in z_ki_in_orbit), 2026-09-27

## Question
M1 starts its orbit as soon as the lift tops out, so the planner height integral (z_ki, card
2026-09-24_planner_offset) is frozen for the whole flight (`traj_t > 0` closes the gate) and the ring
flies 0.05-0.07 m high (R0653: 0.66-0.67; R0668-R0675: z err +0.05-0.07 during the orbit). G1b needs the
ring back within 0.03 m of the target after a 0.1 kg drop. Does integrating through a constant-speed
level orbit remove the offset without new oscillation?

## Change (one variable)
`planner_node._zbias_gated`: with `z_ki_in_orbit:=true` (default false, launch arg in
three_attach_launch.py) the gate also opens when the trajectory is an `orbit` past its 4 s spin-up
(ORBIT_RAMP_S). Every other gate term is unchanged (lift complete, no descent/LAND, |vz_load| < 0.05,
pose fresher than 0.2 s, miss < 0.25 m, all rods taut). `circle`, `line_x`, `fig_8`, `spin` stay frozen.
The orbit has no vertical component, so the integrated miss is the same static offset the hover gate
already integrates. Unit test: test_zbias.py::test_gate_frozen_in_a_trajectory_unless_orbit_opt_in.

## Runs
Bench `m1_rejoin_orbit_fix` (orbit, detach, rejoin, LAND; ~5 min), two arms: z_ki_in_orbit false (R0672
baseline, z err +0.067) vs true, then M1 once if it helps.

## Falsifier
Orbit ring height not within 0.02 m of 0.60 in the last 10 s before the detach, or ring tilt / height
oscillation that the baseline does not have (tilt sd or z sd > 1.5x baseline), or a worse detach/reweld
peak (> baseline + 1 deg), or the integral railing (near_bound warning).

## Risk
The integral keeps state through detach/attach holds (frozen when the gates close, not reset). A fault
that drops the ring is already masked at most by z_i_max (bounded, warns near the bound).
