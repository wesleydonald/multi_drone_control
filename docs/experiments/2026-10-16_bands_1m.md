## Proposed rule (the W4/W5 rule's form, per metric)

Metrics: path RMSE (`payload_rmse_m`) and peak ring tilt (`payload_tilt_peak_deg`), each from the path's start to
LAND, the rig flight read the same way from its bag (`tools/rig_to_run.py`).

  band = [ max(0, min_twin x f_lo - 2 sigma_rig), max_twin x f_hi + 2 sigma_rig ]

- **Factors:** f_lo = f_hi = 1. No rig/twin carry pair exists on today's stack (on 14 Oct the rig carried at 0.125 m/s
  only, `docs/analysis/traj3_2026-10-15.md`), and the W4/W5 rule's own fallback for a class with fewer than 2 pairs is 1.
- **sigma_rig:** the rig's three-carrier steady hover on 14 Oct (RIG-1014-lift3): the sd of the ring's position error
  (for RMSE) and of its tilt (for the tilt), pooled over its steady window. Computed once, before the flight.
- **Scoring:** hit or miss, the band's width, and log(rig / twin midpoint), as W4/W5. Only bands frozen before the
  flight are scored; the naive predictor "rig = twin" (min/max +- 2 sigma_rig) is the same as this band while f = 1,
  so it is not scored separately until a factor exists.

**Alternative:** a fixed factor 2 on both metrics (f_lo = 0.5, f_hi = 2), as the W4/W5 pairs spanned rig/twin
0.85-1.71. Wider, so a hit says less; it guards against the twin's known gaps (no downwash, rigid cables, latency).

**For Wesley:** sign the rule (or the alternative); then the bands are computed and frozen here, before the rig day.

Rule as signed (decisions.md 17 Oct (4)): f = 1, band = [max(0, min_twin - 2 sigma_rig), max_twin + 2 sigma_rig].
How it was read:
- **Window: the path's first moving reference sample (metrics.sweep_window) to LAND,** as the rule's text says, for
  both metrics. The stored `payload_rmse_m` starts at takeoff + 5 s and `payload_tilt_peak_deg` is whole-flight; on the
  rig the lift bob alone reached 12.3 deg (RIG-1014-lift3_3), so the stored keys would fail every rig flight on the lift.
- **Path RMSE:** the ring's 3-D distance to its published reference (10 Hz held, as the rig publishes it).
- **sigma_rig from RIG-1014-lift3_3** (lift3 itself is VOID, QUAD2 had no thrust; lift3_3 is the same lift flown
  again): steady hover at 1.00 m, the last 8 s before LAND. Path error: the sd of the 3-D error norm, 10.3 mm (per axis

  starved lab runs (R1921c-R1936c) are dropped. Hosts mixed: lab PC (RTF 0.35-0.43) and laptop (0.36-0.45).
