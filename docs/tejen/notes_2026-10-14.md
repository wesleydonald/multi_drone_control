## Takeoff race

**What happens.** His MPC took off 0.8 s (t6) and 1.8 s (t6f) before his planner logged TAKEOFF. The two act on different copies of a `ros2 topic pub -t 3`.

**Why it matters.** In WAIT_FOR_TAKEOFF the planner's reference follows the measured pose, with z raised to `min_reference_z`. So QUAD4 climbed to about 0.6 m and drifted 0.4–0.5 m sideways with nothing holding it.

**Workaround on our side:** `ros2 topic pub -w <subscription count>` (t6f2_4 onward). The planner could also hold the latched start XY in WAIT_FOR_TAKEOFF.
