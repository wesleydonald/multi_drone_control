# Rig load and the three-carrier crash (14 Oct 2026)

**Verdict.** The three-carrier lift crashed under the combined stack because our control loops ran late on an
overloaded laptop. Most of the load is display and visual-only work in the hot paths, not control. The cheap cuts below
remove an estimated 200-250 % of CPU without touching a control law. The structural fix is his stack on a second
machine. Registry rows: RIG-1014-lt_5 to RIG-1014-lift3_3, and RIG-1014-t5_2-corr.

## What happened in t5_2 (corrected)

- The creep and pretension were clean. In the lift ramp, the carriers bobbed at ~1 Hz and the bob grew: throttle swung
  0.0-0.5, the carriers moved +-10 cm, and the ring tilt swung 3-16 deg.
- Our trackers ran late for the whole lift: 42-44 Hz with p99 gaps of 37-47 ms (7/8 Oct lifts: 50 Hz, 25 ms).
  - QUAD3's tracker missed ~130 ms (40.97 -> 41.10 s).
  - QUAD2's rod came off its plate at ~41.3 s.
- Attach mode flies with `drop_on_loss` off.
  - Nothing reacted until the 60 deg envelope fault, which disarmed the fleet in the air at ~1 m.
  - QUAD2 still had its carrying thrust and its outward lean. It climbed to 1.57 m, went 1.1 m out, lost power and hit
    the wall.
- QUAD4 and his stack were not involved: QUAD4 stayed on its mat and his TAKEOFF had not been sent.
- The bag's 0.25 s all-topic gap at 40.86 s was the **bag recorder** stalling, not the laptop. The trackers' own logs
  tick through it.

## Beyond the load: the rest of the crash chain

- **Delay margin of the lift.** Exploratory twin batch: the three-carrier attach-mode lift with `SIM_CMD_DELAY_S`
  0 / 0.02 / 0.04, two repeats each.
  - Falsifier: no bob at +40 ms means delay alone does not explain t5_2. Jitter or stalls then become the suspect.
- **The catch after a lost carrier.** `drop_on_loss` is on in dissipative mode and off in attach mode. On, t5_2 would
  have dropped the ring and landed every drone under power: the ring passed 30 deg ~0.1 s before the 60 deg fault.
  Turning it on for attach mode on the rig changes a gate (locked decision): critic plus Wesley's word.
- **The freed carrier.** For the first ~0.2 s after a release, a carrier keeps its carrying thrust and its outward lean.
  This needs a design note before tests 8-9.
- **Magnet hold.** Thursday's test 12 measures how much pull the rod magnet takes.
- **QUAD1/QUAD2 arming.** Their flight controllers' attitude drifts 10-15 deg/min (mocap shows 12-17 deg). Fix with
  `small_angle 180` or an accelerometer calibration (Wesley).

## Our side of Tejen's M2 oscillation review (14 Oct afternoon)

Tejen's review of his own MPC raised three suspects. Checked against our code and today's rig flights:

| Suspect | Our trackers | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Command timing.** A command predicted 100 ms ahead is executed after one tick. | **Present, stronger than his.** tracker_node.py:1886-1887 sends `ocp.get(1, "x")[-4:]`, node 1 of a 20-node, 2.0 s horizon (acados.py:57-58), i.e. 100 ms ahead, every 20 ms tick. The next solve is pinned to that value (`_applied_u`). The throttle can therefore move 0.05 per 20 ms (2.5/s) against the model's 0.5/s (acados.py:140-141; the throttle is index 2 and the comment's label order is wrong). That is 5x; his is 3x at 30 Hz. | Throttle slew, % of ticks above 0.5/s: lift3_3 0-2 %, liftc_3 0-1 % (p95 0.21-0.37/s); **t5_2's bob 19-34 % (p95 0.92-1.14/s, max 2.2/s)**. Dormant in calm flight, active once it oscillates: the controller is more aggressive than its model, which costs damping. |
| **Velocity lag.** The mocap velocity is differentiated on receipt time and low-passed with alpha 0.2. | **Present: the same mocap node.** motion_capture_publisher_node.py:148, 191-203. At ~90 Hz that is ~4 frames = ~45 ms of lag, plus ~5 ms from the differencing. Position is not filtered. The trackers use this twist directly (callback_manager_multi.py:65). | **The twin does not filter**: payload_mocap_emulator.py:124-129 is a plain difference. The twin's controllers therefore see velocity ~45 ms fresher than the rig's, which makes the twin optimistic on damping. |
| **Thrust-ratio adaptation.** | **Not applicable.** kt_trim is off on the rig (locked decision; params.json `kt_trim: false`). The "kT measured in hover" lines are diagnostics only. | - |

**Fix candidates.** Each changes the flight loop, so each needs the twin first (and a critic unless waived):

1. Send the command for the actual next tick: `u0 + u_rate(0) * dt_tick`. Alternatively, make the first shooting interval one tick (non-uniform time steps).
2. Reduce the velocity lag: a short least-squares slope over the last few frames instead of the EMA. Give the twin's emulator the rig's filter, so the twin sees the same lag.

None of these is changed today. Both explain lost damping margin that the load, and anything else that delays a loop, can expose.
