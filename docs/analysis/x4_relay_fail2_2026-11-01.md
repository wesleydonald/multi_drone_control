# X4 relay, the second failure: root cause (1 Nov 2026)

Read-only analysis for the lab chat. Nothing was flown. Runs R3444, R3446, R3448 (relays on the universal hinge, rod
in the plan, `int_k_xy` 0), R3445 (fixed team), R3405, R3407, R3409 (the old setting, stopped by the kick). Scripts,
tables and proposed configs: `results/analysis/x4_fail2_2026-11-01/`. Line numbers are the working tree at 1 Nov
03:00.

Each claim is marked **Logs** (the run's own files, including the world's poses in `gz_record/state.tlog`), **Code**,
or **Inferred**. Times are run.csv seconds (tlog t = run.csv t + 6.37, 7.36, 8.96 s for R3444, R3446, R3448).

## Verdict

**The rejoin did not fail. The ring had already fallen.** The relay's "recharge" is only a clock. The simulated pack
keeps draining, and at about 11.7 V (12.9 V at 1.15 kg) the throttle cap 0.8 can no longer carry the ring on 7
carriers. The ring falls at a release, 34-78 s before that rejoin even starts (R3444: 810.5 s, not 889 s). `run.csv`
is telling the truth: from 848 s the ring lies on the floor. A second defect is independent: the home point ratchets
about 1 cm per fold-in, which falsifies R4 from lap 4 and causes every TASK REFUSED.

**One root cause for the end of the run, one separate defect for the bars, and four lesser ones.** Both main fixes are
config plus one launch line, and neither touches a frozen bar.

**For real flights:** nothing reacts to a ring that is lost slowly (section 4). The ring fell with its tilt at most
29.7 deg, under the drop's 45 deg, and lay on the floor for 190-280 s with the path running. The same planner flies the
rig.

## 1. Why the ring fell: the pack is never recharged

**Code.** With `sim_thrust_map: rig` the plant's thrust depends on a pack model that drains with integrated throttle:
`V_ocv = v0 - 0.022 x throttle-seconds` (`rig_thrust.py`, `PackModel`, `sim_telemetry.py:70`). Nothing ever calls its
`reset()`. A pad dwell resets only the rota's clock in `tools/experiment/relay.py`. The card says "B is a budget, not
a battery model" and "no pack model" (section 3, Known limits), but the plant has one.

**Logs.** Replaying a drone's logged throttle through `PackModel` gives the logged voltage to 0.1 V, over the whole
run.

## 3. Why the tasks are refused: the home point ratchets

**Your first look is right, and the numbers fit to 0.5 cm.**

### The chain

1. **Code:** the planner's horizon keeps previewing the path during a hold. `reference_builder.py:226,248` evaluates
   `traj_t + dt k` whether or not the clock is held. **Logs:** with the clock held at 269.5, node 0's reference is
   (0.101, 0.090) and the horizon's end (0.153, 0.141), moving at 0.078, 0.077 m/s (`RESIZE_TICK`, R3444).
2. **Logs:** so the ring leans about 1 cm along the path's heading (+x +y at the lap's crossing point) in every hold.
3. **Code:** at each fold-in `_attach_ocp` moves the home point onto the measured ring

## 4. From 811 to the fault

**`run.csv` is not stale. Logs:** the world's own poses agree to the digit: ring at (0.321, 0.107, 0.020), tilt 0.0,
from 848 s until the fleet falls on it. 0.02 m is the ring's rest height. `d*_attached` is 0 throughout these runs (it
is not a carrier flag), and `payload_ref_z` 1.0 is only the planner's target.

**What the planner does. Logs:** it plans on. `solve_status` 0 on all 2301 logged ticks after 811 s, n = 7, home
unchanged. The plan reads about 4 N on its busiest rod with the ring on the floor.

**The laps. Logs and Code:** "done" means the planner's clock reached a lap end. After the abort no relay drone is
inside the keep-out and no swap can start, so the only hold left is `traj_lap_hold_s`: 19.2 + 3 = 22.2 s (Logs
22.2-22.3 s). The usual 58 s is that plus a swap. The ring does not move with them.

**The queue. Code:** the runner keeps sending the rota. `TASK` queues (`:3899`). `DETACH` queues (`:1589`) because
/drone_4 is staged (`_relay_away`, `:3523`), and only a blend's end ever releases a queued leave
(`_rejoin_blend_tick`, `:3805-3824`). So none go. The rota's `ATTACH` names the last leaver, not /drone_4, so nothing
retries it (R3448's second try is the rota coming round to that drone again).

**Nothing reacts. Code and Logs:** `drop_tilt_deg` is typed 45 in these configs (`s_chain_pair_noxy.yaml`), and the
tilt peaked at 26.2-29.7 deg, under it and under the 30 deg default too. No rule looks at the ring's height. The start
of a rejoin checks only `lift_progress` (`_rejoin_refused`, `:3578`), so R3448's rejoin starts with the ring on the
floor.

**Which drone tilted, and why. Logs:** /drone_3 in all three (the logs call it Drone 4). With V falling the thrust at
the cap reaches a lone drone's own weight: `19.35 x (0.8 - a + 0.0219 (V - 23.5)) = 5.40 N` at 7.96-8.24 V. **Logs:**
first voltage under 8.2 at about t 1000, and the fault at 7.7-8.0 V. The carriers sink from there.

- **R3444, R3446:** a carrier, the lowest, its rod nearly flat (87 deg off vertical). Its reference goes under its
  rest height, so its tracker latches `on the floor after the flight: armed idle` (`tracker_node.py:1382`). Then it
  tips: 17, 25, 80, 90 deg in 0.4 s (R3444, tlog). `ENVELOPE FAULT` over 60 deg (`:990`) and the fleet disarms.
- **R3448:** the staged drone, on the floor under a reference at z 1.37 m during its 2nd rejoin, moving sideways. It
  reads 78 deg at the fault. Six others had already gone to armed idle without a fault.

**Inferred:** the fault is the last symptom. The envelope is working.

### Defect 1, the pack

- **A (recommended).** A new launch arg `sim_pack_drain` passed as `pack_drain` (`sim_io_launch.py:163`), default
  unchanged, typed 0 in every X4 config. It makes the plant match the frozen card ("no pack model"), with flat thrust
  at about 23.3 V. Against it: the twin's voltage sag (decision 30 Sep) is off for X4, which is a line in the card's
  Known limits. A plant setting, so Wesley's word.
- **B.** A real recharge, the pack reset or ramped at the dwell's end. The plant then shows what the claim says.
  Against it: code, up to 8 V of jump (3 N of thrust unless ramped), an errand must not reset, and no bar needs it.
- **C.** Raise `throttle_max`. One number, but it hides the cause at 12 V (2 V per cell). Not recommended.

### Defect 2, the home point

- **A (recommended).** `attach_datum_shift: false` typed in the X4 configs. The planner already reads it (`:595`), and
  `graph_detach.py` must pass it, one line (only `graph_attach.py:544` does now). Home stays put, pads stay 5 cm
  clear, the first lap's curve stays right, no default changes. Against it: with the old setting a 35-48 mm pull would
  come back at each weld. With the rod in the plan the offset is the steady lean, so no pull is expected (Inferred).
- **B.** Defect 3's fix with the shift kept. It removes the lean too, but it is planner code and a critic for what A
  does in a line.
- **C.** A larger `pad_margin_m`. It only delays the refusals (nearest pad 1.14 m at leave 20), and R4 still falls.
- **D.** Score R4 on the moved curve. R4 would pass, but it is a frozen bar and it hides that the path moved. Not
  recommended.

### The lesser ones

- **Defect 3.** `reference_builder.py:226,248`. Flatten the horizon while the clock is held. It would matter on the

- **Defect 4.** Ring far under its reference for a set time: LAND. A gate, so a critic and Wesley's word on the

- **Defect 5.** The runner ends the run on a REJOIN ABORT, as R1 has fallen by then. It saves wall time only.
