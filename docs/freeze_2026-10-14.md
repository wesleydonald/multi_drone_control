## 1. P1: the frozen texts

### 1.1 T6 release metrics
**Data.**
- Twin: `results/<date>/R####_*/logs/run.csv` and `events.csv`.
- Rig: the same two files from `tools/rig_to_run.py` run with the bag (a 50 Hz grid; the ring pose comes from the bag).
- Rows without a ring pose are dropped. No filter.

**Tilt vector** θ(t), in degrees, world frame:
- n = the ring's body z axis in the world;
- tilt = atan2(√(n_x² + n_y²), n_z);
- θ = tilt · (n_x, n_y) / √(n_x² + n_y²).

So |θ| is the scalar tilt (`payload_tilt_deg`).

**Event time** t_e:

| kind | twin | rig |
|---|---|---|
| unannounced | the runner's RELEASE | RELEASE: the first `/drone_N/magnet` OFF in the bag |
| announced | the runner's DETACH | DETACH: the planner's detach line in the T2 console (`/fleet/detach`) |
| soft (announced, ramped) | the ramp start: the first planner tick after DETACH at which the leaver's planned tension falls | same |

LAND is the LAND event (runner or T2 console). The soft-detach start is not coded yet (Tier A needs it, W4/W5 do not).

**Pre-window:** [t_e − 5 s, t_e).
- θ̄_pre = the mean of θ over it.
- σ_pre = √(mean |θ − θ̄_pre|²).
- **Validity [Q12]:** the ring reference must be still for at least 5 s before t_e. Still means the height within 5 mm
  of its value at t_e, and the speed (a 2 s chord) within 0.02 m/s of its median over the last 2 s. Otherwise the row is
  flagged and still reported.

**Δθ (primary):** the maximum of |θ(t) − θ̄_pre| over t in [t_e, t_end].
- t_end = LAND.
- **[Q1]** When two runs are compared, both are cut at the shorter hold:
  - each 8 Oct pair at the rig flight's event-to-LAND;
  - the W4/W5 bands and the W4/W5 rig values at t_e + 15 s (the D5 minimum hold).

**Beside Δθ:**
- the absolute peak, max |θ| over the same window;
- the scalar rise, the absolute peak minus the mean of |θ| over the pre-window.

The 30° drop rule and the join bars stay on absolute tilt.

**Time back in band:**
- **[Q2]** θ̄_post = the mean of θ over [LAND − 5 s, LAND). It is scored only if LAND comes at least 10 s after t_e.
- The band half-width is b = max(2 σ_pre, 0.5°).
- **[Q3]** t_back is the earliest t at or after t_peak (the time of Δθ) such that |θ(s) − θ̄_post| ≤ b for every sample s
  in [t, t + 1 s], with t + 1 s ≤ LAND.
- It is reported as t_back − t_e. If there is no such t, the row reads "not back".

The other T6 metrics keep their current scorers and are not part of this freeze: dip, detection, steady offset, landing
lean, clearance, the survivors' share, the peak survivor pull and its rate against the hold, contact to proof, and the
throttle step.

### 1.2 Resolution rule (lever pairs)
Arms A and B, each with n ≥ 2 runs **[Q8]**, one value per run (Δθ unless the row says otherwise). A pair is **resolved**
only if all three hold:
1. sign(mean B − mean A) is the frozen sign;
2. the ranges do not overlap (max A < min B, or max B < min A);
3. |mean B − mean A| ≥ max(1°, 2 × the larger range), where range = max − min **[Q8]**.

Otherwise it is **unresolved**.

A "no sign" prediction (§1.5) is **confirmed** when condition 2 or 3 fails. It is **falsified** when 2 and 3 both hold,
in either direction.

### 1.4 Ledger band rule (§4.4 of the thesis)
**The band.** It is computed per rig cell from a twin set {Δθ_twin} flown on the same tree as the rig flight, before it:

  band = [ max(0, min Δθ_twin × f_lo − 2 σ_rig), max Δθ_twin × f_hi + 2 σ_rig ]

**Class factors** come from the 8 Oct rig/twin pairs (table below).
- **[Q5]** Classes are by announcement. A class with fewer than 2 pairs has f_lo = f_hi = 1.
- **[Q6]** Each pair's ratio is ρ = Δθ_rig / mean Δθ_twin. Then f_lo = min ρ and f_hi = max ρ over the class.
- **[Q1]** Both sides of a pair are cut at the rig flight's hold.

**σ_rig [Q4]:** the pooled √(mean |θ − θ̄_pre|²) over the four 8 Oct rig pre-windows. This is the same quantity as
σ_pre.

**Assumption, stated:** W4 and W5 fly the model integral with the freed drone disarmed alone. All four 8 Oct pairs flew
the reference integral. The factors are assumed to carry the rig/twin gap across that change.

**Scoring.** Only rows whose band was frozen before the flight are scored. The 8 Oct pairs are "agreement, not scored".
- Hit: the rig Δθ lies in the band.
- Width: hi − lo.
- Log ratio: ln(rig Δθ / twin midpoint), where the twin midpoint is (min + max) / 2 of the raw twin values.
- **[Q13]** Two naive predictors are scored the same way (hit and width):
  - "rig = twin": [min Δθ_twin − 2 σ_rig, max Δθ_twin + 2 σ_rig];
  - "rig = the previous rig flight of the same cell": [prev − 2 σ_rig, prev + 2 σ_rig]. For W4 that is r303_4 and for W5
    it is r305 (same event cell; integral mode and freed-drone policy differ), labelled as such.

### 1.5 The 1-DOF model and its signs for every Tier A cell
**Model** (thesis §4.5a). Survivor i's feedforward pull is

  F_i = F_i^plan + k (F_i^meas − F_i^plan)

with k = 0 on the planned pull (`cable_source` model) and k = 1 on the measured pull (`cable_source` mocap).

- F^plan jumps to the new shares at the resize. The resize comes at the command (announced), along the unload (soft
  v2), at detection about 0.3 s after the release (unannounced), or never (detection off).
- F^meas follows the true rod pull late by the measurement lag (the 80 ms slope).
- With the model wrong, F^plan after the resize is off by the planner's error (D9).

The ring tilts about the gap by an amount that grows with the size and the duration of the mismatch between the pull the
survivors apply and the pull the ring needs. So the arm with the larger, longer mismatch has the larger Δθ.

The model has no magnet holding limit. It claims nothing about a magnet letting go (r0013). A run in which a magnet lets
go is reported, not scored against it.

**Shared conditions** unless a row says otherwise: 120° (leaver at 90°, survivors at 30/150/270), hover, model integral,
detection on, the week5 candidate tree, n = 3 per arm. "A < B" means Δθ(A) < Δθ(B).
