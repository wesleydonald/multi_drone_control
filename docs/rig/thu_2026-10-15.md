  ... (free hang)`, not fallback.

  one drone: check its FC first.
- Ring drop before the fold-in: GIVE UP. After it, an `UNANNOUNCED DETACH` of drone 3: LAND.
- DISARM only when the console says nobody is in the air (emergencies excepted).
