## Stop rules
- **A magnet lets go unplanned:** with the 3-quad layouts the ring drops and everyone lands, so there's nothing to
  press. Check every magnet before the next flight.
- **Ring tilt over 15 deg, or rocking that grows:** LAND. In a pair, rocking over 15 deg peak-to-peak: LAND.
- **Any tilt over 30 deg (55 deg for two quads):** the trackers drop the ring automatically.
- **A quad that stays ~0.2 m up after the ring is down, then drops:** its thrust map is strong. Note which quad.
