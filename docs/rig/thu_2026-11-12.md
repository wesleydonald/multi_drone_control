## Switches (typed in front; they combine)
| Switch | Scripts | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `BAND=wide` / `BAND=mid` | every attach | his bands back to 8/10 cm, or 6/6 cm (default 5/5) |
| `DROP=arrival` | every pickup | release at the transit height on arrival (no settle). Not recommended: it releases higher (Te-Jen agrees); use FORCE DROP |
| `RELEASE=0.14` | every pickup | his release height (default 0.03) |
| `PHOLD=0` / `PHOLD=0.03` | k1zo, k1z, fm9, fm9c | the pickup hold off, or tighter (default 0.04) |
| `PAUSE=pickup` | fm9c | the circle also at rest for the pickup |
| `OBJ=bare` | every pickup | the object without its funnel (38 mm contact height) |
| `QROD=0.010` | nrtf, nr1f, nro, fm9, fm9c | the bounce comparison (default off) |
| `PBOX=0` / `PBOX=1.5` | every pickup | the pickup box off, or tighter (default 1.9 m) |
