**What is new since 22 Oct**
- **The release button:** RELEASE RIDER on the RViz panel (shown with the magnet rows, so on every rig T1). It names the quad the
  planner plans under 1 N ("/drone_k on plate p"). The first click arms it, a second click within 3 s sends magnet OFF
  at 10 Hz for 1 s. One per flight. No more T4 release lines.
- **The velocity filter:** a T1 switch, `MDC_MOCAP_VEL_FILTER=slope MDC_MOCAP_VEL_DRONES=<carriers>` in front of the T1
  line. Without it, T1 flies the old filter. Changing the filter means a T1 relaunch.
- **Paths (Wesley 22 Oct evening: 0.75 m):** every path has the 5 s hover hold (`params_file:=$C/traj_hold.yaml`).
  The rig's paths are 0.75 m by default (real.yaml): the figure-8 is size 0.75 (x ±0.75, y ±0.375 m) and the lines go
  0.75 m out, so the outboard quad peaks at ~1.475 m. Type no size (a typed `traj_radius` on a figure-8 is refused).
  The spinny line is the constant-rate full turn, now over the shorter trip: ~32 deg/s instead of 24, so expect the
  ring's yaw to trail by ~16 deg (22 Oct: 12 deg at 24 deg/s). The figure-8's corners are a little sharper than at 0.9.
- **Position limit (Wesley 22 Oct):** any quad past ±1.75 m in x or y makes the fleet LAND. It flies only once the
  critic has passed it and its switch is on; this sheet's line will say so (its twin runs fly 22-23 Oct). Start the
  ring at the cage centre: the 22 Oct path starts sat up to 16 cm off.
- **Cables:** 1 m everywhere (`cable_len:=1.0`; the planner measures each cable at handover and logs it).
- **Operating:** RViz (T1 opens it) for ARM, TAKEOFF, LAND and the release. The console (T3) is optional.
- **Planner on the fast cores (Wesley 23 Oct):** `MDC_PLANNER_CPUS=0-7` (in `w5` below) pins the planner to the laptop's
  P-cores; on 22 Oct it ran near its 100 ms tick at 3 quads. `rig_flight.sh` prints it. Read the first flight's planner

## Stop rules
- **A magnet lets go unplanned:** with 3 or 4 quads the ring drops and everyone lands, so there's nothing to press.
  Check every magnet before the next flight.
- **Ring tilt over 15 deg, or rocking that grows:** LAND. For a pair, the see-saw over 15 deg p-p or growing: LAND.
- **Automatic drops:** any tilt over 30 deg (55 for two quads, 45 in Part C) and the trackers drop the ring.
- **A quad that stays ~0.2 m up after the ring is down, then drops:** its thrust map is strong. Note which quad.
