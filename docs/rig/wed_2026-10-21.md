# Wed 21 Oct: three-quad carry (QUAD2, QUAD3, QUAD4), two speeds per shape (10 flights)

Every flight copies the twin's carry claim: three carriers, 0.55 m rods, plates 0 / 4 / 8, the same mode and the same
LAND timing. Each flight therefore scores against its frozen 0.55 m band (`tools/n2_bands.py --rod 0.55`). Speeds are
0.125 and 0.2 m/s. The circle and figure-8 radius is 0.5 m, and the line is 1 m.

**Layout:** the quads go on plates 0, 4 and 8, any quad on any plate, each laid straight out at 0.70 m. QUAD4 needs
the same 0.55 m rod as the others.

```bash

git log --oneline -1                 # note it down

R="tools/rig_flight.sh real_control_launch.py mode:=dissipative num_drones:=3 attach_azimuths_deg:=0,240,120 target_z:=1.0 lift_ramp_vel:=0.15 params_file:=$C/traj_hold10.yaml"
```

  ```bash

  ```
- **T3:** `python3 tools/console.py --drones 3 --no-partner --slot-gate`

`drone_2` QUAD4. The console shows a pack voltage for all three.

## Flights (T2; slow first, then fast only if the slow one was clean)

```bash
$R load_traj:=circle traj_speed:=0.125 c125      # one lap ~47 s; LAND when it hovers again
$R load_traj:=circle traj_speed:=0.2   c200      # ~29 s
$R load_traj:=fig_8  traj_speed:=0.125 f125      # ~28 s; LAND when it hovers again
$R load_traj:=fig_8  traj_speed:=0.2   f200      # ~19 s
$R load_traj:=line_x traj_speed:=0.125 l125      # LAND 40 s after it starts moving (as the twin)
$R load_traj:=line_x traj_speed:=0.2   l200      # LAND at 40 s
$R load_traj:=orbit  traj_speed:=0.125 o125      # LAND at 40 s
$R load_traj:=orbit  traj_speed:=0.2   o200      # LAND at 40 s
$R load_traj:=spin   traj_speed:=0.125 s125      # ~47 s; LAND when it hovers again
$R load_traj:=spin   traj_speed:=0.2   s200      # ~29 s
```

**Every flight:** wait for READY, then ARM and TAKEOFF. The path starts once the lift is done. At the end press LAND,
wait until every quad is disarmed, then Ctrl-C T2.

**Scoring:** I score every flight against its band tonight. Between flights, the summary PNG in
`results/rig/2026-10-21/figures/` is enough.

## Stop rules

- **A magnet lets go:** with three carriers, any loss drops the ring and lands everyone. Nothing to press. Check every
  magnet before the next flight.
- **Ring tilt over 15 deg, or the ring visibly off the path:** LAND, and do not fly the fast run of that shape.
- **The fleet disarms 0.3-0.5 m up just after the ring touches down:** this is the known false touchdown (the fix is
  not on the rig). Check the quads and props, and note the flight name.
- **A quad that stays ~0.2 m up after the ring is down and then drops:** its thrust map is strong. Note which quad.

## Two quads (Wesley, after the three-quad set): QUAD2 on plate 0, QUAD3 on plate 6

Both quads lie straight out at 0.70 m. The ring-tilt drop is at 55 deg (`configs/rig/two_quad_drop55.yaml`), just
under the 60 deg envelope that disarms the whole fleet. Each quad carries half the ring: expect a hover throttle of
about 0.71 against the 0.80 cap. Any loss drops the ring.

```bash

ros2 launch bringup real_io_launch.py num_drones:=2 drone0_serial:=/dev/QUAD2 drone1_serial:=/dev/QUAD3 mocap_drone_body_ids:=12,13 magnet_initial:=ON
# T3
python3 tools/console.py --drones 2 --no-partner --slot-gate
# T2
R2="tools/rig_flight.sh real_control_launch.py mode:=dissipative num_drones:=2 attach_azimuths_deg:=0,180 target_z:=1.0 lift_ramp_vel:=0.15 params_file:=$C/two_quad_drop55.yaml"
$R2 load_traj:=hover h2q          # first: hover; LAND after ~20 s
$R2 load_traj:=circle traj_speed:=0.125 c2q      # only if the hover was clean
```
