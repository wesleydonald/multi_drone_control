## Flying each run
- **1, 2 (hover attach):** his ARM, ARM, TAKEOFF; his TAKEOFF (it waits for the release); steady ~10 s: DETACH QUAD4
  (3 s unload, `PARTNER RELEASE`). Watch `CONTACT`, `ATTACH: proven`, `FLEET RESIZED to n=4`; ~10 s on four, LAND.
  At launch the start line must say `welded at start: ... (plate 9)`.
- **3 (nro):** his ARM, ARM, TAKEOFF; on the circle DETACH QUAD4; let the three circle; then his TAKEOFF:
  `attach pause: the orbit slows to rest`; after the fold-in `attach pause over`; a lap on four, LAND.
- **4 (k1zo):** his ARM, ARM, TAKEOFF; his TAKEOFF once the ring circles steadily. He picks up, climbs straight up,
  comes in along plate 9's line over the moving drop point, matches it, settles, lowers and drops (or, `DROP=arrival`,
  drops on arrival), then lands. **No drop within ~30 s over the ring: QUAD4 magnet OFF, then LAND.** Never LAND while
  QUAD4 holds the object over the ring.
- **5 (fm9c):** as 1 to the release (DETACH on the circle); he picks up and drops into the circling ring (the settle
  drop by default); when he heads back the planner logs `attach pause: ... (his TRANSIT_TO_REATTACH)` and the ring slows
  to rest; re-attach on 9; `attach pause over`; a lap on four, LAND. No drop within ~30 s over the ring: magnet OFF, then
  ON, and he goes on to the re-attach.
- **6 (fm9hc):** before his TAKEOFF, T2 must show his `ASSUMED ATTACHED` (else the object is not on the magnet); then
  as 5 from the drop.
- **Funnel and drop:** the drop fires within 8 cm of the drop point (`drop_on_arrival_xy` 0.08), so the object's edge
  stays inside v4's rim; watch where it lands in the net.

  **DESCENT BAND (read first; Wesley 10 Nov: the recommended set is the default):** with funnel v4 the scripts keep his
bands as flown (start within `match_xy` 0.08 m held 0.5 s; the descent pauses outside `descent_abort_xy` 0.10 m). For the
supervisor's funnel type `BAND=cone` in front of any attach script (`BAND=cone bash ~/rig_nrtf.sh`): 0.05 / 0.5 s / 0.05.
Why (`results/analysis/attach_band_2026-11-10/summary.md`, 32 rig descents of 4-5 Nov): where QUAD4 starts its descent
does not predict where it lands (r = +0.02 over the 3.2 s down); only the pause band acts late enough to aim, and it
cannot aim better than ~2.5 cm. With v4 the as-flown bands would have put the disc fully inside 59 % of the time (19/32),
its centre in the mouth 91 %; tightening below ~4-5 cm brings back "no attempt" (within 2 cm the tip never stayed 0.5 s).
The cone's 2 cm window: est. 36 % fully inside at 0.05 against 21 % measured at today's bands, ~4 attempts a minute.
