**Verdict:** four quads carry the new payload as they carried the old ring. A pair, and the T (which loads like a
pair), carries it only at throttle_max 0.9. At the default 0.8 the twin loses the ring. At 0.9 the pair has about

## Decisions for Wesley (before the chain and the attach)
1. **throttle_max 0.9 wherever a pair or the T 0/3/6 carries the ring.** The chain line (ch1) already types it. The
   attach line `A` (sq1, k1) does not, and its carriers on 0/3/6 load like a pair (below). Recommended: add
   `throttle_max:=0.9` to `A`.
2. **The cable-pull cap for the pair and T stages.** In the twin the pair pulls 12.1-12.3 m/s^2, 2.2 under the cap, so
   the cap never binds and a cap-20 re-fly would change nothing (step 4's 5 % trigger was not met). The rig pulls more
   than the twin for the same load (below), so on the rig a pair at 1.15 kg should read about 13.5-14.6, right at the
   cap. A cap that binds by only 3 % held the twin's ring 17 cm low for the whole hover (R2841), which looks like today's
   17-57 cm rig sinks. The options:
   (a) type `cable_accel_cap:=20` on ch1 and `A`. This is untested on the rig, and the twin cannot test it. It also
   changes a tracker threshold, so it needs your word.
   (b) keep 14.5 and LAND on a sink of more than 10 cm, as the sheet already says.
   (c) LAND after the T instead of releasing the rider.
   No recommendation between (a) and (b): the twin cannot tell them apart.
3. **Attach layout (sq1, k1).** The square's T (carriers on 0/3/6, plate 9 empty) is statically a pair: the quad on
   plate 3 carries almost nothing (pull 0.5 m/s^2, against 12.1-12.3 on plates 0 and 6, R2840/R2842). sq1 holds that
   load for minutes while Tejen flies the transit. The M1 triangle (carriers 3/11/7, QUAD4 on 9) shares the ring three
   ways: the twin's triangle needs 0.69 throttle and pulls 8.3 (R2838). It is also his rig default layout
   (`carrier_azimuths_deg` [90,330,210]). If you keep the square, see decision 1.
