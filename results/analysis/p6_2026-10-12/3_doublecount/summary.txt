a second time on the measured pull")

Made by: python3 tools/resize_doublecount.py   (branch p6-offline; reads logs only, no bag)
Data: the planner log (node-0 planned tension t and its vertical part -t s_z per slot), the trackers' logs (the cable
term they applied at node 0, acx/acy/acz, which on cable_source mocap is the measured rod pull; the model throttle
u2), the T2 console's FLEET RESIZED line, events.csv. Rig: r302_3 (announced), r303_4 (unannounced) as asked, plus
r304_3 and r305; twin, measured pull, 7 Oct trees: R1118, R1121 (announced), R1140, R1145, R1139, R1141 (unannounced).
Full table: doublecount.csv, table.md (one row per survivor).

Method: per survivor, the plan's vertical step (first resized row against the 2 s before the release) beside the
measured vertical pull's change from that same baseline, read at the resize; then the commanded thrust (m kT u2)
across the resized plan's arrival at the tracker (mean over 0.08-0.12 s after the first resized log row's stamp,
minus the 60 ms before it).

Answer:
- Does the measured term already contain the change the plan adds? On an unannounced release, mostly yes: by the
  resize (0.33-0.42 s after the release) the survivors' measured vertical pull had moved by 60-152 % of the plan's
  step on the rig (median 93 %) and 55-105 % in the twin. On an announced release, no: the plan's step comes with the
  OFF, and the measured term has not moved (rig -9 to 15 %, twin about 0 %).
- Is the change then counted a second time? Not at the tick. On the unannounced releases the survivors' commanded
  thrust did not take the plan's step again when the resized plan arrived: in the twin it moved by 0.39 N or less;
  on the rig it moved by 0.14-1.40 N AGAINST the step's sign in all 9 survivors, the start of a swing of about 1 Hz
  (in r303_4 and r305 the drone whose planned pull fell gains 1.0-1.3 N of measured pull within 0.14 s while the two
  whose pull rose lose up to 0.5 N; 0.44 s later it has reversed). On the announced releases the plan's step reaches the thrust within 0.1 s, before the load does
  (twin 59-76 % of it, 6 of 6; on the rig 0.2 s before the magnet opened, mixed in sign).
- On the two rig hovers released unannounced, the tilt rise peaked before the resized plan reached the trackers, so
  the resize cannot have set their dtheta.

What it means for the thesis: the double-count variant frozen beside the 1-DOF model (T9 off < on, T10 unannounced <
announced) does not have the mechanism it names in these logs. The frozen signs stand (they are frozen); the text
should not explain a twin or rig order by a double count. What the logs show instead: an announced step leads the
load (in the twin and on the rig), and on the rig a counter-swing starts at the plan's arrival.

Caveats:
- One rig flight per kind (two more unannounced flights as context); the twin rows are the 7 Oct trees, not the
  candidate.
- The measured term is the tracker's applied value (slew-limited, after the 80 ms filter); its vertical part is
  compared, because the plan's horizontal pull and the rig's ~0.55 N inward force mix in the horizontal.
- The rig's counter-swing is not explained here. A candidate: the resized plan lowers the reference of the drone
  whose pull falls by about 4 cm (r303_4) and 5.6 cm (r305) within 0.35 s, and the measured term (the drone's
  acceleration minus its thrust model) reads that motion back as rod pull. Not tested.
- The twin console is on wall time, so the twin's resize is taken as 0.1 s before the plan's arrival.
