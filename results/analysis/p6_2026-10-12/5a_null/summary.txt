P6 item 5a: the rig's dtheta null and the minimum detectable effect at n = 3

Made by: python3 tools/rig_null.py --convert   (ROS sourced for --convert; tools/release_bands.py computes every dtheta)
Data: every 7 and 8 Oct flight that flew the ring (17), as tools/rig_to_run.py run directories made with the bag; the
ones the main tree had not converted were built in the p6-offline worktree's results/rig_runs (not committed).
Outputs: null_pseudo_events.csv (every pseudo-event), null_summary.csv, mde_power.csv, tables.md.

Method: pseudo-events every 0.5 s wherever the ring reference had been still for 5 s and stayed still, with nothing
happening (no release, slip, LAND or fleet stop), for the window W after it; dtheta exactly as T6 (the tilt vector's
largest departure from its 5 s pre-window mean). W = 7 s (the 8 Oct hovers held 6.6-7.7 s) and 15 s (the W4/W5
horizon). Then 20000 simulated pairs of arms through the frozen resolution rule (freeze 1.2), noise from the null.

At a true difference of 0.8 deg (the ramp's twin effect) the rule resolves 1-2 % of pairs at n = 3 (mde_power.csv).

What it means:
- dtheta has a floor on the rig: with nothing happening, the T6 statistic reads about 5 deg (hover, 7 s) to 6 deg
  (path, 15 s), about three times the window's own rocking (sigma_pre), because it takes the largest excursion of a
  2-D vector over the window.
- Against that floor: r302_3 (11.2) and r304_3 (16.9) are far above it on any reading. r303_4 (5.3) is inside the raw
  hover null but above every null window once scaled by its own, smaller rocking (4.5 sigma_pre against a null
  median of 3.25). r305 (8.0, circle) and r0012 (6.1) are not separable from the null when scaled (3.4 and 3.1
  sigma_pre, against null medians of 3.1-3.3): their transient is no larger than the ring's own rocking over a
  window of that length.
- A rig lever pair at n = 3 resolves only differences of about 4.5 deg (hover) to 7-9 deg (path), and more if real
  repeats vary more than the null (they will: the release falls at a random phase of the rocking). The ramp's 0.8 deg
  is far below it, as the plan expected.
- Under the frozen rule the minimum detectable effect grows from n = 2 to n = 3 (3.7 to 4.6 deg on the hover null):
  the bar is twice the larger range, and the expected range of three runs is 1.5 times that of two, while the means
  sharpen only by 1.2. More repeats make a pair harder to resolve.

Caveats:
- The hover null rests on two flights (r000014, which carried 100 g extra, and r002), both on 7 Oct (carry planner,
  model integral), not the 8 Oct detach configuration; no 8 Oct hover was still for the 12 s a 7 s pseudo-event needs.
  The 8 Oct pre-release windows rocked less (sigma_pre 1.0-1.4 deg against 1.3-1.8), so the raw hover null overstates
  theirs; the scaled reading corrects for that if the null grows in proportion to the rocking (it does across the
  null's own flights: 2.8-3.6 sigma_pre).
- Pseudo-events 0.5 s apart overlap; the effective sample is about the number of flights.
- The noise models are run-to-run variability lower bounds; no rig cell has two releases yet.
