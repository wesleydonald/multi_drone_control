P6 item 5b: the stretch-detector sweep (false alarms over all bags; delay against the threshold from the releases)

Made by: python3 tools/slip_replay.py --sweep --dates 2026-10-07 2026-10-08   (ROS sourced; read-only)
Data: the 45 bags of 7 and 8 Oct (17 flew the ring), with their planner logs. Outputs: sweep_false_alarms.csv,
sweep_delays.csv, tables.md.
Method: the flown rule (dissipative_node._detect_tick: over max(typed, measured rod) by the threshold on two ticks in a
row, a 3 cm jump since the last tick within it, nothing when every cable is over at once) replayed on the planner's
own logged distances at its 10 Hz ticks, with the threshold from 2 to 12 cm, over each flight's active time (the
planner phase before LAND or the fleet stop; with drop_on_loss, until the load is down). The 3 cm jump guard is held.

False alarms (fires for a drone that had not let go):
| threshold (cm)       | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
|----------------------|---|---|---|---|---|---|----|----|
| fires, 17 flights, 660 s active | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
The largest rod error of a drone still on its plate, at the bag's full rate (item 2's flights.csv): 2.3 cm in normal
flight (r0005), 2.8 and 3.2 cm only inside the r200006 and r0013 capsizes.

Delay from the release to the fire (ms; release = the OFF, or the slip onset for an uncommanded one):
| threshold (cm) | r303_4 (OFF) | r304_3 (OFF) | r305 (OFF) | r0013 /drone_1 (slip) | r200006 /drone_1 (slip) |
|----------------|--------------|--------------|------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|
| 2-4            | 410          | 332          | 337        | 155                   | 169                     |
| 5-6            | 410          | 332          | 337        | 155                   | 278                     |
| 8              | 545          | 332          | 476        | 275                   | 278                     |
| 10-12          | 545          | 454          | 476        | 275                   | 278                     |
Not in the table: r0013 /drone_2 slipped 75 ms before the fleet abort (no two ticks fit); r200006 /drone_2 slipped
after LAND (the node no longer detects); the announced leavers (r0012, r0013, r302_3) were already resized.

What it means: no threshold from 2 to 12 cm made a false alarm in 660 s of rig flight, so 6 cm has margin to spare;
but lowering it below 6 cm would not have caught the 8 Oct releases sooner: a released rod stretches from under 2 cm
to over 6 cm within one 100 ms tick, so the delay is set by the magnet's opening (0.1-0.2 s), the tick and the
two-tick rule (one more tick, 100 ms), not by the threshold. Raising it to 8 cm costs 0.13-0.14 s on two of the
three. On the two slips, 2-4 cm would have fired one tick sooner on r200006.

Caveats:
- 17 flights, 660 s; a 0-of-660 s false-alarm count bounds the rate only loosely (one per 220 s at 95 %).
- The jump guard is part of the rule held fixed here; without it a 2-3 cm threshold sits on the 2.3-3.2 cm errors above.
- The replay uses the node's own logged distances, as flown; where the flight had already resized the leaver away, the
  T2 line's cable and then the bag stand in (tools/slip_replay.py).
