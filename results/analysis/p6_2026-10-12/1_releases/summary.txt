What the freeze does not define, and what this table does instead (labelled in event_rule / window_end):
- Uncommanded release (r200006): no magnet OFF and no DETACH exist, so the freeze has no event time. t_e is the
  slip detector's onset in the bag (the rod's pivot-to-plate distance 3 mm over its baseline, in the run that
  passes 3 cm). Not a frozen time.
- No LAND after the event (r0013 capsized; r200006's second slip came 2.2 s after LAND, in the descent): the
  freeze's window end (LAND) does not exist or precedes the event, so the window ends at the fleet abort.
- Time back in band is scored only when LAND comes >= 10 s after the event (freeze Q2). Only r305 qualifies.
  r0012 (8.0 s) and the 8 Oct hovers (6.6-7.7 s) landed too soon; the capsizes never landed. t_back_2s is a
  labelled variant (post tilt over the last 2 s, end >= event + 4 s), not the frozen metric.
- r200006: the pre-window is flagged (Q12): slip 1 came 3.8 s into the orbit, inside its 4 s speed ramp;
  slip 2's pre-window holds slip 1 and its 30.6 deg excursion (sigma_pre 8.6 deg), so its dtheta is not a rise
  from a steady state.
