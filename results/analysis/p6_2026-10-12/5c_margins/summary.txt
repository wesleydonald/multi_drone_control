Made by: tools/admissibility_map.py (rig-prep 5889052, results/analysis/admissibility/removals.csv: metrics.cog_margin
on the plates at radius 0.225 m), filtered to the three four-carrier rig layouts and marked with the flights that
flew each loss (margins.csv). No new computation; the rig rows of item 1 carry the same margin per event.
Margin = the distance from the ring's centre to the nearest edge of the survivors' polygon, r cos(gap / 2) for a
largest gap under 180 deg; negative when the centre lies outside.

What it means: every commanded rig release left the survivors a positive margin (112.5 mm on the 120 deg gap,
58.2 mm on the 150 deg gap); the one uncommanded release that the ring survived (r200006, 30.6 deg) left zero margin,
and the second one left two carriers. Of each four-carrier layout's four possible losses, only one keeps the centre
strictly inside: the N-1 rule holds for the designed leaver alone, never for an arbitrary magnet.

Caveat: the margin assumes the ring's centre of mass at its geometric centre; no rig measurement of the offset exists.
