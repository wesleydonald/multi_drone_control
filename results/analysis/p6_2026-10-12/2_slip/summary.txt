Two detectors exist in the code, and both were replayed:
1. The slip detector (tools/drop_trigger_replay.py, the source of "a survivor let go at +0.155 s"): at the bag's
   full mocap rate (~90 Hz), each rod's pivot-to-plate distance against its own length. The rod is rigid, so a
   magnet that holds keeps the error within a few mm. A release is |rod error| > 3 cm on 6 samples in a row; its
   onset is the first sample 3 mm off the baseline. Commanded releases are timed from their magnet OFF; any other
   sustained error of a drone still on its plate is an uncommanded release (or a glitch, if it comes back).
2. The stretch detector that flies (dissipative_node._detect_tick, identical at the 8 Oct code b811d90 and on
   rig-prep): at each 10 Hz planner tick, a cable over max(typed, measured rod) by 6 cm on two ticks in a row,
   having jumped 3 cm. Replayed on the planner log's own distances (as flown) and on the bag (cross-check).

check, ARM failures, the r303 creep timeout). No flight had a fleet stop (disarm, abort or envelope fault) before
LAND.

is on 7 Oct, with the rods planned at 45 deg: r0013's two survivors and r200006's two. The detector that flies

Caveats:
- The record holds four uncommanded releases in two flights: r0013 lost two survivors (+0.155 s at 2.6 deg of
  tilt, and +0.397 s at about 46 deg, inside the capsize) and r200006 two. The plan's "three in two flights"
  counts r0013's second one out; if that is deliberate (a consequence, not a cause), the text should say so.
- "Commanded" is judged from the OFF command alone; whether an OFF release took the magnet longer than usual
  cannot be told apart from a slip at the same moment.
- 30 Sep had no bags; its lift-phase releases (6 magnets in 3 flights) are not in any replay.
- The bag replay reads the poses at the planner's tick start, estimated as the log stamp minus the solve and
  30 ms (calibrated on the three 8 Oct detection ticks, where the bag then gives the cable lengths the T2 lines
  printed). Its ~20 ms jitter can move a fire by one tick on a fast-stretching rod (r0013's first slip: 275 ms
  on the bag against 155 ms on the planner's own distances); the replay on those distances has no such error.
